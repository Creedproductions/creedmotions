FAQ

Common Questions

Before you reach out — these might answer it faster.

How long does a website take to build?
Most websites are delivered in 2–7 working days depending on the package. Package 1 (landing page) takes 2–3 days. Package 4 (full web app) can take longer depending on scope — we'll confirm a timeline at quotation stage.
Do you work with clients outside Uganda?
Yes. We work with clients remotely across Africa and beyond. All communication and file delivery can be handled fully online via email, WhatsApp, or video call.
What do I need to provide before work starts?
We need your content (text, images, logo, and brand colours) before we begin. If you don't have content, we can assist with copywriting and logo design as add-ons. The more you provide upfront, the faster we deliver.
Will I own the website after it's built?
Yes — completely. You receive all source files, credentials, and assets. There's no ongoing fee unless you choose Mode C. We don't lock you in.
Can you update my existing website?
Yes. We take on redesign and maintenance work for existing sites. Contact us with details of your current site and what you need changed and we'll quote accordingly.
How does payment work?
We work on a 50/25/25 split — 50% before work begins, 25% at design approval, and 25% before the site goes live. We accept Mobile Money and bank transfer.
